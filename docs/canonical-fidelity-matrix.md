# Canonical fidelity matrix

Task 009C freezes the eight-operation V0.1 model. `PASS` means the technical scene is a defensible reconstruction within the stated evidence boundary. `PARTIAL` means the semantic correction still renders, but a generic representation, inaccessible normative geometry, or unresolved large-range behavior prevents a canonical claim. No status below is a certification claim.

Source keys:

- **JIS table** — JIS Z 8208:2007 Table 1 via the accessible Kikakurui reproduction, cross-checked with the Japan Printing Industry Federation chart. Normative table not directly verified from the paid JSA edition.
- **GB table** — GB/T 14706-1993 items 1-21 and Appendix A via the public five-page scan; identity and current status verified at SAMR. The scan is not a digitally signed official distribution.
- **CNS table** — CNS 3927 / Z7050, edition 1996-09-26, inspected through the official CNS Online preview; identity, edition, and current status verified through CNS Online and the BSMI 2026 catalog. Redistribution permission has not been obtained, so scans and diagrams are not published.
- **Korean teaching** — Incheon Office of Education teacher guide (2026), p. 35, citing the Ministry of Education 2025 Grade 6-1 Korean textbook; terminology and product forms cross-checked with Hancom Office documentation. KS A 0104 table not directly verified.
- **US institutions** — Oregon Appellate Courts Style Manual (2002), printed p. 117; MIT OCW proofreader's marks (2012); Caltech thesis guidelines (2024), Appendix C; GPO Style Manual (2016), 1.22 where relevant.

## Cross-cutting limits

- Inline, one-line cases are the canonical V0.1 surface. Wrapped and multi-rect ranges use a documented generic fallback unless a row says otherwise.
- Runtime geometry now receives measured `font-size`, `line-height`, and source `font-family`. Stroke width is `0.092em`, clamped to 1.15-2.35 px; labels are `0.72em`, clamped to 10.5-18 px; inserted and replacement material is `0.9em`, clamped to 11.5-26 px.
- Technical rendering is canonical. Editorial rendering may vary stroke character but not topology, anchor, or relation semantics.
- Spacing semantics are intentionally asymmetric: `increase-spacing` targets one `text-point` boundary, while `decrease-spacing` selects a `text-range` containing existing whitespace. The horizontal specimen bench exposes this contract beside the native mark pair for every pack.
- Shared number-sign or close-up topology is independently justified per notation pack; it is not evidence that CJK and US proofreading share one universal spacing grammar.
- Dedicated `cancel-correction` specimens expose the stable `cancel → referenced correction` ID relation in technical appearance only. This metadata documents the model and is not part of any canonical proofmark construction.
- The same-English mixed proof is an engineering layout stress fixture. Native-language gallery fixtures, not that dense comparison, are the canonical cultural specimens.
